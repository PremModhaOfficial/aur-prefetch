Progress, better than nom

Question: what should the progress screen look like so it beats nix-output-monitor? Five structural candidates, selected and crossed, then hillclimbed one change at a time.

The bar: nom's tree is excellent at states, structure and timers. It is passive, Nerd-Font/colour dependent, and it does not tell you how to repair a failure. Canopy+ keeps the tree and adds causality, keyboard, ASCII and a failure lens.

Failure state

Repair, not retry

nom shows the failed build. Canopy+ shows the last line, the repair command and whether pacdiff ran. That is the gap between a monitor and an operator.

Why it beats nom

  • Causal edges say what is blocking, not just the tree shape.
  • Keyboard: focus, filter, log, ASCII.
  • Failure lens with a repair path and no blind retry.
  • Post-run receipt survives the process.
  • Timers are measured; ETA is only shown when local history exists.
